My Approach

AI only drives a return
if it's set up correctly.

AI is an amplifier. Point it at a clear, organized business and it compounds everything — your capacity, your output, your margins. Point it at chaos and you get faster chaos.

Out of the box, it doesn't know your clients, your voice, your pricing, or the dozen things you do every week that only live in your head. That's why most people try it, get generic answers back, and quietly decide it isn't for them. It isn't a tooling problem. It's a foundation problem.

So we don't start with tools. We start with what you're actually building, get the pieces organized, and then build the AI layer on top.

The goal of all of it is capacity. I take a human-centered approach to AI — it runs your back office, not your voice — so your time goes to the work that actually matters, and your life and business both run with more ease.
